Open your shop from photos
01

Your photos become listings

A vision model looks at each photo and writes the title, description, price and the words shoppers search for. It flags what a photo can't show, like size or brand, so you check those before you publish.

Each product is then registered with PayPal.

02

Your shop page tells AI shopping assistants what you sell and how to order, through four actions: search, look closer, prepare a checkout and check an order. Other AI apps can connect to the same actions from outside the browser.

WebMCP on the shop page · MCP at /mcp/<your-shop>

AI assistants can shop your stall

03

The buyer pays in PayPal

Shoppers press Buy with PayPal on any product, or let an assistant get the order ready. Either way, the buyer signs in to PayPal and approves the payment, and the money is captured on their way back.

You see every order, how it came in and, for an assistant's order, each step it took.
