Open your shop from photos

Add a photo of each thing you sell, up to six. The AI writes a first draft of every listing. You check them, press Publish, and your shop is open.

The AI only knows what it can see in a photo. Fix anything it got wrong, and check the points it flagged, before your shop goes live.

Buyers can pay with PayPal, and AI shopping assistants can find your products and get orders ready. Every payment is approved by the buyer in PayPal.
